4. Cycle & Soak Calculator

Denver has mostly heavy clay soil (the default). Clay absorbs water slowly — about 0.10 in/hr. Spray heads deliver up to 1.5 in/hr, so water sheets off the surface before soaking in. If your yard was re-graded with imported fill, or you have sandy areas near the foothills, select a different type.

Set each zone's Slope in the zone rows above (default: Flat). Then click Calculate — the tool will find the ideal short cycle time for each zone so water soaks in instead of running off. Times auto-fill in your zone rows and appear on the printed card.
