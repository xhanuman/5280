Our technicians understand these micro-variations and adjust system programming and component selection accordingly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: My Sloan's Lake home was built in the 1940s. Can my old sprinkler system be upgraded or should I replace it entirely?

**A:** It depends on the system's current condition. Many older systems have sound main lines but outdated heads, valves, and controllers. We can often retrofit these systems with modern, efficient components at a fraction of the cost of full replacement. However, if the pipes are galvanized steel showing significant corrosion, or if the layout no longer matches your current landscape, a new installation may be more cost-effective long-term. We provide honest assessments and help you choose the best option for your budget and goals.

### Q2: I notice soggy spots in my yard even when the sprinklers have not run recently. Is this a leak or something else?

**A:** Near Sloan's Lake, soggy areas can result from either irrigation leaks or a naturally high water table. We begin by conducting a system pressure test with all zones off to determine if water is escaping your pipes. If the system holds pressure, the moisture is likely groundwater. In that case, we can recommend drainage solutions or adjust your watering schedule to prevent over-saturation. If we detect a leak, we pinpoint its exact location to minimize excavation.

### Q3: How do I comply with Denver Water's watering restrictions?

**A:** Denver Water typically allows residential watering three days per week, with specific days assigned based on your address. Watering should occur before 10 AM or after 6 PM to reduce evaporation. We program your controller to meet these requirements while optimizing run times for your specific soil type and sun exposure. Smart controllers can automatically adjust for weather conditions, ensuring compliance even when restrictions change seasonally.

### Q4: Construction is happening on my street. Could it be affecting my sprinkler system?

**A:** Absolutely. Heavy equipment can cause ground vibrations that crack pipes or dislodge fittings. Utility work can also affect water pressure. If you notice new issues coinciding with nearby construction, contact us for an inspection. We can document any damage, which may be useful if you need to file a claim with the construction company or city.

### Q5: When should I schedule winterization in Sloan's Lake?

**A:** We recommend scheduling your blow-out service between late September and mid-October. Colorado weather is unpredictable, and an early freeze can catch unprepared systems. Winterization removes all water from your pipes, valves, and backflow preventer, preventing freeze damage that can cost hundreds or thousands of dollars to repair in spring.

### Q6: What makes smart controllers worth the investment?

**A:** Smart controllers connect to weather data and soil moisture sensors to water only when needed. In Denver's semi-arid climate with unpredictable rain, this prevents both overwatering and underwatering. Most homeowners see 20-30% reductions in water usage, which pays for the controller within a few seasons while maintaining a healthier lawn.

### Q7: Do you service the newer townhomes and condos around Sloan's Lake?

**A:** Yes. We work with individual homeowners, HOAs, and property managers throughout the neighborhood. For multi-unit properties, we can coordinate system-wide inspections and maintenance programs that ensure consistent care across the entire community.
