## Frequently Asked Questions: Wash Park Irrigation

### How do I know if my older Wash Park home has galvanized irrigation pipes?

Signs include rusty water when the system first runs, reduced pressure over time, and frequent leaks at fittings. We offer free inspections to assess your system's pipe materials and recommend cost-effective upgrade paths.

### Will sprinkler repair damage my mature landscaping?

Our technicians use minimally invasive techniques specifically developed for established landscapes. We hand-dig near tree roots, use directional boring when possible, and always restore disturbed areas to their original condition.

### How does the water table near Washington Park affect my sprinkler system?

High groundwater can cause pipes to shift and create stress on joints. We install flexible couplings and proper drainage solutions to accommodate seasonal water table fluctuations common near the park.

### What smart controller do you recommend for Wash Park homes?

We typically recommend Rachio or Hunter Hydrawise controllers for their Denver Water compliance features and ability to create complex zone schedules that account for Wash Park's varied shade conditions.

### How often should I have my Wash Park sprinkler system inspected?

Given the age of most Wash Park systems and the high value of neighborhood landscaping, we recommend comprehensive inspections every spring and a mid-season check in July to catch issues before they damage your lawn.

### Can you help me reduce water bills while maintaining my landscape?

Absolutely. Many Wash Park homes see 20-30% water savings after we optimize head placement, fix hidden leaks, upgrade to high-efficiency nozzles, and install smart controllers that adjust to weather conditions.

### Do you offer emergency repair services in Wash Park?

Yes. We understand that a broken main line or stuck valve can quickly damage a Wash Park lawn worth thousands in landscaping investment. We offer same-day emergency service for urgent situations.
