## Saddle Rock's Unique Sprinkler Challenges: Soil, Systems, and Standards

Maintaining an efficient sprinkler system in Saddle Rock requires understanding the specific conditions that affect irrigation infrastructure in this southeast Aurora community.

### Expansive Bentonite Clay: Southeast Aurora's Primary Challenge

Saddle Rock sits on some of Aurora's most challenging **expansive bentonite clay soil**, which creates significant stress on irrigation systems:

1. **Severe Ground Movement:** Expansive clay swells dramatically when wet and contracts when dry, creating ground shifts that crack PVC pipes, displace sprinkler heads, and damage valve boxes.
2. **Slow Water Absorption:** Clay soil absorbs water at approximately 0.1 inches per hour, requiring shorter, more frequent watering cycles to prevent runoff while achieving adequate root penetration.
3. **Foundation Protection:** Many Saddle Rock homeowners must balance lawn irrigation with foundation watering needs, as improper moisture levels around foundations can cause structural issues.

Our technicians understand how to program systems for **cycle-and-soak** watering that works with Saddle Rock's clay while protecting your home's foundation.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Why does my Saddle Rock lawn have brown spots despite regular watering?

**A:** Saddle Rock's expansive clay often prevents water from reaching roots effectively. Common causes include: compacted soil blocking absorption, misaligned heads creating coverage gaps, clogged nozzles from mineral deposits, or system components degraded after 15+ years of use. We perform comprehensive audits to identify and resolve the specific cause.

### Q2: How should I water with Saddle Rock's clay soil?

**A:** Use **cycle-and-soak** programming: water in 2-3 short cycles (8-10 minutes) with 30-60 minute breaks between cycles. This allows clay soil to absorb water gradually without runoff. Early morning watering (4-6 AM) reduces evaporation and wind drift loss while complying with Aurora Water guidelines.

### Q3: What are Aurora Water's current watering restrictions for Saddle Rock?

**A:** Aurora Water typically implements three-day-per-week watering schedules during summer months, with watering prohibited during peak afternoon hours. Smart controllers with local weather integration help maintain compliance automatically. We program all systems to meet current Aurora Water regulations.

### Q4: When should I schedule sprinkler winterization in Saddle Rock?

**A:** Southeast Aurora's elevation means freezes can occur earlier than expected. Schedule winterization **between late September and mid-October**. Our professional blowout service uses high-volume, low-pressure air to safely clear all water from pipes and the backflow preventer to prevent freeze damage.

### Q5: My HOA sent a violation notice about my lawn. Can you help?

**A:** Yes. We provide rapid response for HOA compliance issues throughout Saddle Rock. We'll diagnose why your lawn isn't receiving adequate water, make necessary repairs, optimize your system for uniform coverage, and provide documentation of completed work for your HOA records.

### Q6: How can I reduce my Aurora Water bill while keeping my lawn green?

**A:** We achieve savings through: fixing hidden leaks (a single broken line can waste 6,000+ gallons monthly), installing Smart Controllers that qualify for Aurora Water rebates, upgrading to high-efficiency nozzles, and programming cycle-and-soak schedules optimized for Saddle Rock's clay soil.
