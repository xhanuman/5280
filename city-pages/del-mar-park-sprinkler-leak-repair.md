## Del Mar Park's Unique Sprinkler Challenges

Maintaining an efficient irrigation system in Del Mar Park requires specialized knowledge of the neighborhood's construction era, mature landscaping, and infrastructure considerations.

### 1970s-1980s Irrigation Systems: Age and Material Concerns

Del Mar Park homes were built during an era when irrigation technology and materials differed significantly from modern standards:

| Era-Specific Issue | Impact on Your System | Our Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Galvanized Steel Pipes** | Corrosion, rust buildup, reduced water flow, and eventual leaks | Replacement with modern PVC or poly pipe sections |
| **Brass Fittings** | Dezincification causing brittleness and failure at connection points | Upgrade to durable modern fittings and couplings |
| **Original PVC (Schedule 40)** | 40-50 years of soil movement and UV exposure causing cracks | Targeted pipe section replacement and leak repair |
| **Outdated Controllers** | Mechanical timers with limited programming, no weather adjustment | Smart controller upgrades with Aurora Water rebate eligibility |

Many Del Mar Park systems are operating well beyond their expected lifespan of 20-25 years. While some components may still function, efficiency has often degraded significantly, leading to higher water bills and inadequate coverage.

*   **Changed Coverage Needs:** Landscapes that have evolved over 40+ years may have outgrown original sprinkler head placements
*   **Foundation Plantings:** Established shrub beds near foundations require careful water management to prevent structural issues

Our technicians specialize in adapting aging irrigation systems to work harmoniously with Del Mar Park's beautiful mature landscapes.

Older systems are prone to hidden leaks that waste thousands of gallons monthly. We use electronic leak detection equipment to pinpoint problems in:

*   Main supply lines (often original galvanized or early PVC)
*   Lateral zone lines running under established lawns
*   Valve manifolds and connection points

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: My Del Mar Park home has the original sprinkler system from the 1970s. Does it need to be completely replaced?

**A:** Not necessarily. While 40-50 year old systems typically need significant attention, complete replacement is often unnecessary. We assess each component individually, replacing failed or inefficient parts while preserving functional infrastructure. Many homeowners save substantially through targeted repairs and upgrades rather than full system replacement.

### Q2: I have large trees that seem to be affecting my sprinkler system. What can be done?

**A:** Tree root intrusion is common in Del Mar Park's mature neighborhoods. We can locate where roots have damaged pipes, repair or reroute affected sections, and install root barriers where appropriate. We also adjust head placement and coverage to account for shade patterns created by mature canopies.

### Q3: How do I know if my old system has hidden leaks?

**A:** Warning signs include: unexplained increases in your Aurora Water bill, wet spots in your yard between waterings, areas of unusually green grass, reduced water pressure in certain zones, or the sound of running water when the system should be off. Our electronic leak detection service can pinpoint hidden problems without excavating your entire yard.

### Q4: Should I upgrade to a smart controller in my older Del Mar Park home?

**A:** Absolutely. Smart controllers are one of the most cost-effective upgrades for older systems. They automatically adjust watering based on local weather data, helping you comply with Aurora Water restrictions while potentially qualifying for rebates. Modern controllers work with existing valves and wiring in most 1970s-1980s installations.

### Q5: When should Del Mar Park homeowners schedule winterization?

**A:** Schedule your blowout between **late September and mid-October**, before the first hard freeze. Aurora's unpredictable fall weather means early scheduling is essential. Our professional service safely removes all water from pipes, valves, and the backflow preventer to prevent costly freeze damage.

### Q6: My water bill has increased significantly. Could my sprinkler system be the cause?

**A:** Very likely. In established neighborhoods like Del Mar Park, aging irrigation systems are a primary cause of high water bills. Common culprits include: slow leaks in deteriorating pipes, heads that run constantly due to worn seals, valves that fail to close completely, and outdated controllers that overwater. A system audit typically identifies significant savings opportunities.
