## Cherry Hills Village-Specific Irrigation Challenges

### Large Estate Property Considerations

Cherry Hills Village estates present unique irrigation demands unlike any other Denver-area community:

- **Extensive Zone Coverage**: Properties commonly require 15-30+ irrigation zones spanning multiple acres of turf, gardens, and specialty plantings
- **Multiple Controller Systems**: Larger estates often utilize two or more controllers working in coordination to manage the complete property
- **Long Mainline Runs**: Irrigation mains stretching hundreds of feet require proper sizing, pressure management, and strategic leak detection
- **Specialized Landscape Areas**: Separate zones for putting greens, equestrian facilities, tennis courts, and pool surrounds demand precision irrigation

### Dual Water Source Management

Many Cherry Hills Village properties utilize both municipal water and private wells:

- **Denver Water Service**: Properties connected to Denver Water must comply with all regulations, restrictions, and backflow requirements
- **Private Well Systems**: Numerous estates supplement or rely entirely on private wells, requiring specialized pump integration, filtration, and pressure regulation
- **Hybrid System Coordination**: Managing systems that draw from both sources requires sophisticated controller programming and zone prioritization
- **Water Rights Considerations**: We understand the complexities of Colorado water rights as they apply to residential irrigation

## Frequently Asked Questions: Cherry Hills Village Irrigation

### Our estate has over 25 irrigation zones across multiple acres. Can you manage a system this large?

Absolutely. Large estate systems are our specialty. We service numerous Cherry Hills Village properties with 25-40+ zones managed by multiple controllers. Our technicians are trained on commercial-grade equipment and understand the unique challenges of coordinating irrigation across extensive acreage, including pressure management, zone sequencing, and water window optimization.

### We have both Denver Water service and a private well. Can you integrate both sources?

Yes. Dual water source systems are common in Cherry Hills Village, and we have extensive experience integrating municipal water and well systems. This includes proper backflow protection, pump controls, filtration requirements, and controller programming to prioritize water sources based on availability and cost.

### How do you locate leaks on a property this large?

We employ professional-grade leak detection equipment including acoustic listening devices, pressure decay testing, and thermal imaging. For properties with extensive mainline networks, we use systematic pressure isolation testing to identify leak zones before any excavation. This approach protects your landscape investment while pinpointing problems efficiently.

### Can you work with our estate manager or groundskeeping staff?

We regularly coordinate with estate managers, groundskeepers, and property management teams throughout Cherry Hills Village. We can provide staff training on controller operation, establish maintenance communication protocols, and deliver detailed documentation for property records.

### What should we budget for maintaining an estate irrigation system?

Annual maintenance for a typical Cherry Hills Village estate ranges from $1,500-$4,000+ depending on system complexity, acreage, and the number of zones. This typically includes spring activation, mid-season audits, and winterization. Given that estate landscapes often represent $100,000+ investments, professional maintenance provides significant protection for this value.

### Do you offer monitoring services for large systems?

Yes. We can install flow sensors and connect your system to monitoring platforms that alert you to potential leaks, system faults, or unusual water usage patterns. For properties with multiple controllers or well systems, this provides valuable oversight and early problem detection.
