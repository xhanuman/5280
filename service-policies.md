## 1. Service Scope

We may identify other issues while turning on your system. These will be priced additionally based on circumstances. If you suspect an issue, please make note when scheduling your appointment.

## 2. Leak Detection

Our leak detection add-on service focuses primarily on valve leaks (this is not included in a basic sprinkler startup service). Leaks in lateral lines may exist and those are beyond the scope of the leak detection service we offer.

**PLEASE take time to review your irrigation system after we depart.** Check inside the valve box periodically - ask us and we'll teach you how to open them. While exposed supply line leaks are typically visible, underground leaks may not be detectable during our brief visit.

**We strongly recommend installing leak detection devices in your crawlspace or basement and testing them quarterly.**

---

## 3. System Vulnerabilities

Pressure fluctuations in water systems can cause older plastic parts to fail without warning, particularly exposed PVC or poly pipes. Ground settlement, roots, and other factors may also lead to pipe failure.

Many older systems have very thin-walled PVC main line pipes which become brittle as they age. Previous freeze damage can also affect underground pipes if they had water in them for whatever reason after a winterization in the past, even several years later. We recommend full replacement for aging systems.
